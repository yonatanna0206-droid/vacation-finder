# Project: TripSync



## 1\. Project Overview



* **What it is:** "TripSync is an app for finding the perfect vacation for you."

* **Target audience:** "The app is designed for anyone looking to find a vacation tailored to their preferences."

* **Main goal for the user:** Many people want to go on vacation but struggle to choose the right destination. With so many options, varying prices, and different types of trips, it’s hard to know which location truly matches their personal preferences, such as budget, travel style, and weather.
TripSync solves this problem by collecting the user's personal preferences, analyzing them, and recommending the vacation destinations that best fit their needs.

* **Site language \& direction:** English, LTR
